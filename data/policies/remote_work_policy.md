# Remote Work Policy
Document ID: POL-RW-2024-01
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Owner: People Operations
Last Reviewed: October 15, 2024

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines eligibility, expectations, equipment, and security requirements for remote and hybrid work at the company. It applies to full-time and part-time employees in roles designated as remote-eligible by their manager and People Operations. The goal is to support flexible work while protecting productivity, collaboration, and information security. This policy does not create a contractual right to remote work; arrangements may be modified or revoked when business needs change.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees may request a remote or hybrid arrangement after completing six (6) months of continuous employment, unless an offer letter explicitly states remote eligibility from day one. Contractors and temporary workers are not covered unless a statement of work says otherwise. Managers must confirm that the role can be performed effectively without daily on-site presence, that customer or compliance obligations can still be met, and that the employee has a reliable private workspace. People Operations reviews complete requests within ten (10) business days and records the approved arrangement in the HR system. Employees on active performance improvement plans are generally ineligible until the plan is successfully closed.

## 3. Work Arrangements
Approved arrangements fall into two categories: fully remote (no required office days) and hybrid (at least two (2) on-site days per week at an assigned hub). Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in the employee’s local time zone unless a team charter defines different hours. Employees must be reachable on approved channels during core hours and must keep their calendar status accurate. Hybrid employees should schedule on-site days to overlap with teammates when practical. Cross-time-zone teams should document handoff expectations in writing. Temporary remote work for travel or personal reasons lasting fewer than ten (10) business days may be approved by a manager without a formal policy change.

## 4. Equipment and Stipend
The company provides a standard laptop, headset, and access to required software. Fully remote employees receive a home-office stipend of seventy-five dollars ($75) per month for internet and peripherals. Hybrid employees are not eligible for the monthly stipend but may expense approved one-time setup costs up to two hundred dollars ($200) with receipts. Lost or damaged company equipment must be reported within forty-eight (48) hours. Employees must return all company property within five (5) business days of separation. Personally owned devices may be used only if enrolled in mobile device management where required by IT.

## 5. Security Requirements
Remote workers must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems, enable full-disk encryption, and keep endpoint protection enabled. Sharing accounts or forwarding confidential documents to personal email is prohibited. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with VPN connected. Screens should be positioned to avoid shoulder surfing in shared spaces. Security incidents, including lost devices or suspected phishing, must be reported to IT Security immediately. Periodic security awareness training is mandatory for all remote-eligible staff.

## 6. Performance and Attendance
Remote status does not change performance expectations, quality standards, or response-time norms for the role. Employees must attend mandatory all-hands and team meetings unless excused in advance. Repeated unavailability during core hours, missed deliverables, or failure to collaborate asynchronously may result in revocation of remote privileges after a written warning. Managers should document coaching conversations and involve People Operations before permanently changing an arrangement.

## 7. Travel to Hub Offices
Fully remote employees may be required to travel to a hub office up to four (4) times per year for planning, onboarding support, or training. Reasonable travel costs are reimbursed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-EX-2024-03). Employees should book travel through the preferred portal when available and submit expenses within the deadlines in that policy.

## 8. Policy Violations and Questions
Violations of security or attendance requirements may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination. Questions about eligibility, stipends, or exceptions should be directed to People Operations at people@example.com. IT Security handles questions about VPN, device management, and incident reporting.
