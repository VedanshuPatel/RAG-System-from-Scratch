# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document ID: POL-EX-2024-03
Effective Date: June 1, 2024
Owner: Finance
Version: 2.0 (current)
Last Reviewed: November 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose
This policy describes which business expenses are reimbursable, required documentation, spending limits, coding rules, and submission deadlines. Employees should spend company funds prudently and only for legitimate business needs. This Version 2.0 document replaces earlier expense guidance, including POL-EX-2023-03.

## 2. Eligible Expenses
Reimbursable expenses include airfare, ground transportation, lodging, client meals, conference registration, and required job-related training. Shipping of company equipment, visa fees for business travel, and internet costs while traveling overnight for work may also be reimbursable with receipts. Personal entertainment, commuting to a regular office, companion travel, gift cards for personal use, and luxury upgrades (for example, first-class airfare without pre-approval) are not reimbursable. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client entertainment events within the meal limits and where permitted by local law and customer policy.

## 3. Receipts and Documentation
Itemized receipts are required for any expense of twenty-five dollars ($25) or more. Credit-card statements alone are not sufficient. For client meals, note attendee names, companies, and business purpose on the report. Missing receipts require a missing-receipt affidavit and manager approval; repeated missing receipts may result in denial. Foreign-currency expenses should be converted using the rate on the transaction date from the corporate card feed or a documented public rate source.

## 4. Spending Limits
- Meals while traveling: maximum fifty dollars ($50) per person per day without manager approval.
- Lodging: book standard rooms through the preferred travel portal; nightly rates above three hundred dollars ($300) need pre-approval from a director.
- Local client meals: maximum seventy-five dollars ($75) per person, capped at four (4) company attendees unless a director approves a larger group.
- Ride-share or taxi to and from airports is reimbursable; rides for personal errands are not.
- Domestic economy airfare is the default; premium economy or business class requires VP approval except for flights longer than eight (8) hours.

## 5. Section Code Reference
When coding expenses, use section code **EX-MEAL-50** for travel meals under the daily cap and **EX-LODG-STD** for standard lodging. Conference fees use **EX-CONF**. Using outdated codes (for example, legacy meal code EX-MEAL-100) may delay reimbursement and will be returned for correction by Finance.

## 6. Submission Deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the expense date. Reports submitted after sixty (60) days require Finance Director approval and may be denied. Employees should submit partial reports after each trip rather than waiting to batch many months of spend.

## 7. Approval Workflow
Managers approve reports under one thousand dollars ($1,000). Reports of one thousand dollars ($1,000) or more also require Finance review. Employees must not approve their own expenses. Approvers should verify business purpose, coding, and limit compliance before approving. Spot audits are conducted quarterly.

## 8. Card and Cash
Corporate card holders should charge eligible expenses to the card whenever accepted. Out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursed by ACH within fifteen (15) business days after final approval. Personal charges on a corporate card must be repaid immediately and may lead to card suspension.
