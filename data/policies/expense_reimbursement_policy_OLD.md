# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document ID: POL-EX-2023-03
Effective Date: January 15, 2023
Owner: Finance
Version: 1.0 (OUTDATED — retained in shared drive by mistake)

## 1. Purpose
This policy describes reimbursable business expenses and limits for employees traveling or purchasing goods for company work. It was the controlling Finance guidance throughout 2023. A newer policy (POL-EX-2024-03 Version 2.0) took effect on June 1, 2024; this file should have been archived but remains discoverable in the knowledge corpus, which can produce conflicting answers.

## 2. Eligible Expenses
Travel, meals, lodging, conference fees, and job-related training are reimbursable with manager approval. Home-office furniture was briefly reimbursable under a 2023 pilot and appears in some older examples, though that pilot ended. Personal entertainment and commuting costs are not reimbursable.

## 3. Receipts
Receipts are required for expenses above fifty dollars ($50). Below that threshold, employees may submit without itemized proof unless Finance requests documentation during audit. Client meals should list attendees when practical.

## 4. Spending Limits
These limits are from Version 1.0 and **conflict with the current policy**:
- Meals while traveling: maximum **one hundred dollars ($100)** per person per day.
- Lodging: nightly rates up to **four hundred dollars ($400)** without pre-approval.
- Local client meals: maximum **one hundred twenty-five dollars ($125)** per person.
- Domestic flights may be booked in premium economy without VP approval when the fare difference is under two hundred dollars ($200).

## 5. Section Code Reference
Use section code **EX-MEAL-100** for travel meals and **EX-LODG-FLEX** for lodging. These codes were retired when Version 2.0 introduced **EX-MEAL-50** and **EX-LODG-STD**.

## 6. Submission Deadline
Submit expenses within **forty-five (45) days** of the expense date. Late submissions were commonly accepted with manager email approval under this older process.

## 7. Approval Workflow
Managers approve all reports under two thousand dollars ($2,000). Finance reviews only exceptions and international travel packets.

## 8. Note on Data Quality
This document was replaced by POL-EX-2024-03 Version 2.0 on June 1, 2024. The outdated copy remains in the knowledge base on purpose for this lab, creating conflicting meal caps ($100 vs $50), lodging thresholds, deadlines, and section codes. Prefer the current policy when both are retrieved.
